Method

From feed to row in three moves

1

Start with the promoted set

The public boost list is the raw pool of candidates. Anything that lives on another chain is dropped before it costs a request.

2

Let the deepest pool speak

A token can trade in many pools. The one holding the most liquidity stands in for it, so thin copies never skew a row.

3
